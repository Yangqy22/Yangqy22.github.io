I am a Ph.D candidate at [The Center for Brain-Inspired Computing Research (CBICR)](https://www.cbicr.tsinghua.edu.cn/), Tsinghua University. I am supervised by Prof. [Rong Zhao](https://faculty.dpi.tsinghua.edu.cn/zhaorong/en/index.htm) and Prof. [Luping Shi](https://faculty.dpi.tsinghua.edu.cn/shiluping/en/index.htm). My research interests include brain-inspired computing, neurorobotics and robot learning, particular whole-body control and mobile manipulation. 

My expertise centers on the synergistic inergration of edge computing and perception. This encompasses a range of engineering implementations, including algorithm deployment and acceleration, software-hardware co-design, and robot operating system. Currently, my research emphasizes developing algorithms inspired by mechanisms of the human brain to build intelligent robots.
